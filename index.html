Methodology and units

Source: NASA FIRMS (LANCE), VIIRS near-real-time active-fire detections. Counts are thermal anomalies per UTC day, not burned area; high-confidence uses the FIRMS confidence flag; FRP is fire radiative power in megawatts. NRT values can be revised. The most recent day is partial until the day ends.

Prediction framework

  • Model methodology documented
